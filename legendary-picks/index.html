OVERVIEW

Legendary Picks is a sports data and prediction product for following live games, player performance, props, league standings and esports. Live scores, box scores and play-by-play across the NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL and more. Fast, clean, no noise.

It started as a fantasy layer on top of NBA Top Shot. When Top Shot moved into that space itself, I pivoted Legendary Picks into an independent, data-first sports product that doesn't depend on anyone else's platform.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Live scores across 8+ leagues, with box scores, play-by-play and game detail pages.
  • Predictions: pick winners and track your accuracy over the season.
  • Props: player prop outcomes, hit rates and trends.
  • Esports: CS2 pick'em and fantasy.
  • NFL tools: a mock draft room and draft board built from published ADP.
  • Game previews written only from what's actually true of the game: no stale standings, no filler.

HOW IT'S BUILT

The browser never scrapes anything. Explicit ingest jobs are the only things that write data. They pull from each league's own publisher, resolve players, teams and games by stable source IDs rather than display names, and publish snapshots to a canonical SQLite store. A FastAPI service reads from that store, and the Next.js app reads from the API. If data is missing or unverified, the site says so instead of making something up.

STEP 01IngestLeague sources and data releases pulled by scheduled jobs.
STEP 02StoreOne canonical SQLite store, written only by ingest.
STEP 03ServeFastAPI routers for games, players, props, news and esports.
STEP 04ShowNext.js pages for scores, leagues, players, props and drafts.
