OVERVIEW

For my birthday in 2025 I made a website instead of a party invite. The Warm Fuzzy Wall is a public wall where people share how we met or a fond memory they have of me. Every post is a little warm fuzzy.

Today’s my birthday! I made a warm fuzzy wall website for people to share how we met or a fond memory they have.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Open the link and write how we met, or a memory you have of me.
  • Posts show up on a shared wall for everyone to read.

HOW I BUILT IT

I built it with Replit Agent. It was part birthday present to myself and part experiment in how fast you can go from an idea to a deployed app when an AI agent does the typing. A couple of weeks later I recorded a walkthrough demoing the app and how it was made.
