OVERVIEW

For my birthday in 2025 I made a website instead of a party invite. Warm and Fuzzies is a wall where people post notes about how we met or a fond memory they have of me. Share positive messages and compliments; make someone’s day a little brighter.

WHERE IT COMES FROM

If you don’t know what warm fuzzies are, Posse (my scholarship org) had an annual retreat and we would post cute notes to each other on the wall, and at the end of the retreat you would go home with all your notes. I always wanted to test out a digital warm fuzzy wall.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Write your warm and fuzzy message.
  • Pick a sticky-note color.
  • Add who it’s to, and sign it or stay anonymous.
  • See a live preview of your note, then post it to the wall.
