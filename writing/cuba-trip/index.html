OVERVIEW

I studied abroad in Cuba during a Maymester at UT, during the short window when the country opened up to American visitors. I blogged every single day of the trip.

Some of what stuck with me: Havana's architecture, 1950s American cars with Apple stickers on them, the classic Cuban film posters from the '60s and '70s, $2 mojitos and the warm water at Varadero.

Beautiful people, beautiful country. I'm thankful I got to go when I did.
