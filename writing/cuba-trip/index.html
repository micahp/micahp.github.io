OVERVIEW

In 2017 I studied abroad in Havana for a Maymester and blogged every day of the trip: the Museum of the Revolution, Guanabo Beach, Fábrica de Arte, the Instituto Superior de Arte, lectures on race in Cuba, the Champions League final at a café, and what it’s like trying to get online. Honest, unedited, written the night of.
