OVERVIEW

Inner Monolog is where I write about mental models and unpack the thoughts of thought leaders like Naval Ravikant and Kapil Gupta. It began in late 2019 as a near-daily newsletter, a writing habit modeled on gppls daily but with words instead of songs, and it's still where my longer personal essays go.

Before it was Inner Monolog, it was a blog called Budget Nudist.
