OVERVIEW

Inner Monolog is where I write about mental models and unpack the thoughts of thought leaders like Naval Ravikant and Kapil Gupta. Rawness is authenticity; raw plus polished is a medium-rare mind. It began in November 2019 as a near-daily writing habit, modeled on gppls daily but with words instead of songs.
