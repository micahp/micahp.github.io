OVERVIEW

Innovative Hype is my newsletter. It started in 2019 as a place to think out loud about the music industry and the platforms artists use to reach fans. It grew to cover prediction markets, AI, sports business, crypto and the economy, and in 2023 it added a podcast where I chop it up with builders and creatives.
