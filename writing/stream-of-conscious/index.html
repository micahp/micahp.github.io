OVERVIEW

I started blogging on WordPress, then moved to Tumblr, where I posted daily streams of consciousness: whatever was on my mind, written in one go and published without editing.

The habit stuck. It became my morning reflection, the backbone of Inner Monolog, and eventually a tool: the Stream of Conscious Writer, which deletes your words if you stop typing.
