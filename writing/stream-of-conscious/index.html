OVERVIEW

Daily Streams of Consciousness was my Tumblr: whatever was on my mind, written in one go and published without editing. Most entries open the same way, asking whether yesterday was a W, and then go wherever the thought goes: money, music, fans, discipline.

The habit became my morning reflection, the backbone of Inner Monolog, and eventually a tool: the Stream of Conscious Writer, which deletes your words if you stop typing.
